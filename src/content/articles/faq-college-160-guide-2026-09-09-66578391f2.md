## Understanding the 2026 Skilled Migrant Category Points System

The Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) in New Zealand awards points based on several factors, including qualifications, skilled employment, and age. To be eligible for an application, you must meet a threshold of 160 points. The system is designed to select individuals who are likely to contribute to New Zealand's economy and society.

## How Your Study Field Can Contribute to Points

Your study field primarily affects your qualification points. New Zealand recognises qualifications at various levels, and higher-level qualifications (such as bachelor's degrees, master's degrees, and doctorates) typically earn more points. Additionally, some fields may be considered “skilled” if they lead to occupations that are in shortage or are listed on the skills shortage lists. While the exact points for each qualification level are not detailed in the evidence, it is clear that the level of your qualification is a key factor in reaching the 160-point threshold.

## Reaching 160 Points: What Else Matters

Aside from your qualification, points are also awarded for:
- Skilled employment in New Zealand: holding a job that is assessed as skilled.
- Age: younger applicants receive more points.
- Work experience outside New Zealand.
- Job offers or current employment in a skilled role.

To reach 160 points, you need to combine points from these factors. For example, a bachelor's degree plus skilled employment and a suitable age could already put you close to 160.

## Choosing a Major with Migration in Mind

When selecting a study program, consider fields that lead to skilled occupations with high demand in New Zealand. While the evidence does not specify particular majors, it is reasonable to focus on areas where the country has skill shortages. However, ensure that your chosen qualification is at the right level to contribute to your point score.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the points threshold for New Zealand Skilled Migration?

The threshold is 160 points under the 2026 Skilled Migrant Category.

### Does my study field directly give me points?

Your study field determines the level of your qualification, which in turn affects the points you receive for qualifications. Some fields may also be linked to skilled employment opportunities.

### Can I reach 160 points without a master's degree?

It is possible, as points are also awarded for skilled employment, age, and other factors. A bachelor's degree combined with skilled work and a suitable age could help you reach the threshold.

### What other factors contribute to points?

Skilled employment in New Zealand, age, and work experience are also considered.
