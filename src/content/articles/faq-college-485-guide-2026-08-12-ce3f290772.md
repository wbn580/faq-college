## What Is the 485 Second Temporary Graduate Visa?

The 485 Temporary Graduate visa is a pathway for international students who have completed their studies in Australia. For those who study in regional areas, a second visa may be available, extending the time you can stay and work in Australia.

## How Long Is the 485 Second Visa?

The duration of the 485 second visa depends on where you studied and lived. Specifically, if you studied in a regional area and meet certain conditions, you may be eligible for an additional one to two years on top of your initial 485 visa.

## What Are the Application Requirements?

To apply for the 485 second visa, you must have held, or currently hold, a first 485 visa. You also need to have lived and studied in a regional area while holding that visa. The regional area must be one that is designated as such by the Australian government.

## How to Use the 485 Second Visa to Gain Work Experience

The 485 second visa provides extended work rights, allowing you to gain valuable professional experience in regional Australia. This extra time can help you build your career, network, and potentially transition to other visa pathways. To make the most of it, focus on finding employment within your field of study, and consider opportunities that align with your long-term goals.
