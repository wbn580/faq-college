## Understanding the 2026 SMC Points System

New Zealand's Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) uses a points-based system to assess eligibility for residence. In 2026, the system requires applicants to meet a threshold of 160 points. Points are awarded for various factors, including age, skilled employment, qualifications, and work experience in New Zealand.

## The Role of Study Majors in Earning Points

Your study major can directly influence the number of points you earn under the SMC. Certain qualifications are recognised as being in areas of absolute skills shortage or are linked to occupations that are in high demand. If you choose a major that leads to a skilled occupation and meets the criteria for bonus points, you can more easily accumulate the required 160 points.

## Majors That Align with Skilled Occupation Categories

While specific point values depend on your individual circumstances, majors that lead to occupations on New Zealand's skill shortage lists — such as engineering, healthcare, ICT, and trades — can provide additional points. These qualifications often qualify you for bonus points for skilled employment or for having a qualification in a recognised area.

## Choosing a Study Major for Migration Purposes

When selecting a study major with migration in mind, consider how it maps to the SMC points criteria. Look for degrees that are NZQF levels 7 or higher, are linked to occupations in shortage, and can lead to skilled employment in New Zealand. Your course selection should also consider the duration and the institution's accreditation, as these can affect the points you may claim.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum points required for SMC in 2026?

In 2026, the minimum points required under the SMC is 160.

### Can my study major alone earn me enough points?

Your study major is one component, but the points system considers other factors such as age, skilled employment, and work experience. A major that leads to a skilled occupation can significantly contribute, but it is not sufficient on its own without meeting other criteria.

### Are all study majors treated equally for points?

No. Qualifications that are linked to occupations in shortage or that are at higher NZQF levels can earn additional points, whereas other majors may not. It depends on how your qualification aligns with the points criteria.

### Does the institution I study at matter for SMC points?

Yes, the institution's accreditation and the qualification's recognition can affect your eligibility for points. Ensure your qualification is listed as a recognised qualification under the New Zealand Qualifications Framework.
