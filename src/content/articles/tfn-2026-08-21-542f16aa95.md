## Australia's 2026 Minimum Wage

As of 2026, the Australian statutory minimum wage is set at a specific hourly rate, which applies to most employees across the country. The exact figure is determined by the Fair Work Commission and is reviewed annually. For the most accurate and current amount, always refer to the official Fair Work Ombudsman website.

## Work Hours for International Students

International students in Australia are permitted to work up to 48 hours per fortnight during term time. This limit is set by the Australian government and applies while your course is in session. Outside of term time, you may work unlimited hours. Always check your visa conditions for any specific restrictions.

## Applying for a Tax File Number (TFN)

To work in Australia, you need a Tax File Number (TFN). You can apply online through the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) website. The application is free, and you will need to provide identification documents. Once you receive your TFN, provide it to your employer so that tax is withheld at the correct rate.

## Annual Tax Return Filing

In Australia, the financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June. You must lodge a tax return each year, usually by 31 October, if you have earned income. As an international student, you may be eligible for a tax refund if you have paid too much tax during the year. You can lodge your return online via myTax on the ATO website, or use a registered tax agent. Keep records of your income and expenses to support your return.
