## Understanding the 2026 Skilled Migrant Category Points

The Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) is New Zealand's main points-based residence pathway. Under the 2026 scoring system, applicants need to reach at least 160 points to be invited to apply. The points are awarded for factors such as age, skilled employment, work experience, and qualifications.

## How Study Majors Affect Your Points

Your choice of study major can directly influence the points you earn under the SMC. Certain fields are more likely to lead to skilled employment, which is a major source of points. For example, studying in areas with high demand can improve your chances of securing a job that qualifies for skilled migrant points.

## Selecting a University and Program

When choosing a university and program with immigration in mind, focus on programs that:

- Lead to occupations listed as skilled in New Zealand.
- Offer strong industry connections and internship opportunities.
- Are recognized by New Zealand qualifications frameworks.

Researching the job market and aligning your studies with in-demand skills can help you build a profile that meets the 160-point threshold.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which study majors are most likely to help me reach 160 points?

Majors that lead to skilled occupations in high demand, such as those in health, engineering, and information technology, are more likely to result in skilled employment and thus higher points.

### Does the specific university matter for immigration points?

Yes, the qualification must be recognized by the New Zealand Qualifications Authority (NZQA) to count for points.

### Can I claim points for work experience gained before studying in New Zealand?

Work experience can contribute to your points, but it must be in a skilled occupation and meet the requirements set by Immigration New Zealand.

## Conclusion

Meeting the 160-point threshold under the 2026 SMC requires careful planning. By choosing a study field that leads to skilled employment and selecting a recognized program, you can improve your chances of achieving residence in New Zealand.
