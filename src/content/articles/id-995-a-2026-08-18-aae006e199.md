## Overview of Form ID995A

Form ID995A is the application form for a Hong Kong student visa. It must be completed accurately and submitted along with supporting documents. This guide walks through each section and explains what to prepare for financial proof and accommodation.

## Section-by-Section Filling Guide

### Personal Particulars

Provide your full name exactly as it appears in your passport, along with your date of birth, nationality, and travel document details. Ensure all names match your passport to avoid delays.

### Contact Information

Enter your current mailing address, phone number, and email. Use the address where you can receive mail for at least the next few months, as the Immigration Department may send correspondence there.

### Proposed Stay and Study Details

State the duration of your proposed stay, which typically covers the period of your study programme plus a reasonable period for arrival and departure. Name the university and the course you have been admitted to, including the expected start and end dates.

### Declaration and Signature

Read the declaration carefully, then sign and date the form. An unsigned form will not be processed.

## Preparing Supporting Materials


![Student preparing financial documents for a visa application](https://img.ulec.com.cn/留学-大学FAQ/id-995-a-2026-08-18-aae006e199-image-02-2026-1679x1300.jpg)

### Financial Proof

Financial proof demonstrates that you have sufficient funds to cover tuition fees, living expenses, and other costs during your stay. Common documents include bank statements, scholarship award letters, or financial guarantee letters. Make sure the documents are recent, clearly show your name or the sponsor’s name, and are in a language the Immigration Department can verify.

### Accommodation Arrangements

Include evidence of where you will live in Hong Kong, such as a dormitory confirmation letter from your university, a tenancy agreement, or a letter from a relative or friend confirming you will stay with them. The document should state the address and the duration of your stay.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

Ensure all completed sections are legible and consistent. Do not leave required fields blank; if a section does not apply, write “N/A”. Double-check that your name and passport number match your passport exactly. Supporting documents should be in English or accompanied by certified translations.

## Final Checks and Submission

Before submitting, review every page for accuracy and completeness. Make a copy of the entire application and all evidence for your records. Submit the signed form and supporting documents to the Immigration Department as instructed by your university or the official guidance.
