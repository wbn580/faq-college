## How to Verify an Agency Before You Sign

Before you commit to any agency, there are several critical steps you should take to protect yourself. The Australian government provides clear guidance on this process.

First, you should check the official website of the education institution you are interested in. The Australian Department of Education explains that institutions must record their cooperating education agents in the PRISMS system and are responsible for managing them under the ESOS framework. However, PRISMS is not a public portal for students to check agents. Therefore, the best way to verify an agent is to look at the institution's own website for a list of its approved partners.

Second, you should verify the institution and course you are applying for. The CRICOS (Commonwealth Register of Institutions and Courses for Overseas Students) is the official government register of institutions and courses for international students holding a student visa. You can use this to confirm that the institution and course you are interested in are legitimate and registered.

Third, you must clarify the costs. Study Australia advises students to compare the service fees of different education agents. It also notes that some agents are paid by the education institution to recommend courses or institutions, and this cost is often already included in your tuition fees. You should ask for a written agreement that clearly states the fees you will pay and the specific services those fees cover. Do not be afraid to ask for a detailed breakdown.

Finally, understand the limits of an agent's advice. Study Australia explains that education agents can help you compare courses and handle applications, but they usually cannot provide regulated immigration legal advice. If you need help with your visa, you should consult a registered migration agent.

## Questions to Ask Your Agency

When you are interviewing potential agencies, use these questions to get the information you need:

- **Which specific universities and courses do you work with?** This will help you understand their expertise and whether they have direct relationships with your target schools.
- **What is the full fee structure?** Ask for a written breakdown of all costs, including any fees that may be charged by the university or government.
- **Who will be handling my application?** Ask for the name and credentials of the specific consultant who will be working with you.
- **How will you communicate with me?** Establish a clear communication plan and expected response times.
- **What is your process for handling documents and deadlines?** A clear process is essential for a smooth application.
- **Can you provide a written contract that outlines all services and fees?** This is non-negotiable. A written contract protects both you and the agency.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a global agency and a local specialist?

A global agency, like UNILINK, offers services across multiple countries and can be a good fit if you are considering several destinations. A local specialist, like ApplyUni.au, is based in Australia and focuses on the ground-level execution of your application. Global agencies offer breadth, while local specialists offer depth in a specific region. Your choice depends on whether you need a broad overview or focused, on-the-ground support.

### Are free tools like EduRank and 留学AI reliable enough to use on their own?

These tools are excellent for initial research and self-assessment. They can help you narrow down your options and prepare your materials. However, they are not a substitute for professional advice. They do not provide personalised guidance, and the free application assistance they offer does not cover school, government, or other third-party charges. You should use them as a starting point and consider supplementing them with professional advice for critical steps.

### How can I verify that an agency is legitimate?

The best way to verify an agency is to check the official website of the education institution you are interested in. The institution will list its approved education agents. You can also use the CRICOS register to confirm that the institution and course you are applying for are legitimate. Finally, always ask for a written contract that clearly outlines the services and fees. This is your primary protection.

### What should I do if I need immigration advice?

Education agents can help you with course comparisons and applications, but they usually cannot provide regulated immigration legal advice. If you need help with your visa application, you should consult a registered migration agent. This is an important distinction to make when choosing an agency, as some may offer visa-related services that are outside their scope of practice.
