## New Zealand’s Strategy for International Education Growth

New Zealand is actively pursuing growth in its international education sector through a strategy that emphasizes diversification of source countries and a focus on high-quality, sustainable education offerings. The government’s International Education Strategy aims to rebuild and reshape the sector, recognizing that international education contributes significantly to the country’s economy and global connections. Central to this approach is the idea that New Zealand offers a unique educational experience, rooted in its world-class institutions, safe environment, and distinctive cultural heritage.

## New Zealand Is Not Positioned as an Australian Alternative

Education New Zealand, the government agency responsible for promoting the country’s education internationally, has explicitly stated that New Zealand is not marketing itself as a substitute for Australia. While both countries compete for international students, particularly from markets like China and India, New Zealand’s brand is built on its own strengths—such as a student-centered learning approach, research opportunities, and a welcoming society. Officials emphasize that the country’s value proposition stands independently, and it does not rely on comparisons with its neighbor to attract students. This distinction is an important part of the messaging to prospective students and education agents, ensuring that New Zealand is seen for what it is, rather than what it is not.

## FAQ

### What is New Zealand’s approach to attracting international students?

New Zealand focuses on diversifying its international student recruitment by targeting a range of source countries, rather than depending on a few large markets. The strategy also stresses the quality of education and the overall student experience, from safety to lifestyle.

### Why does New Zealand say it is not an alternative to Australia for study?

According to Education New Zealand, positioning the country as an Australian alternative would undermine its own distinct brand. New Zealand’s education system and culture are promoted in their own right, without direct comparison to Australia, to ensure that students choose it for its unique attributes.
