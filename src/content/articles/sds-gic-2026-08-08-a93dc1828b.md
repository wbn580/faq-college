## Key Updates for 2026

The Student Direct Stream (SDS) is a fast-track study permit process for international students applying to study in Canada. For 2026, the requirements have been updated.

- **IELTS Score Requirement**: The minimum IELTS score required for the SDS channel remains at an overall band score of 6.0, with no individual band lower than 6.0. This applies to the Academic version of the test.

- **Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) Amount**: The required GIC amount has been increased. Applicants must now provide proof of a Guaranteed Investment Certificate of CAD $20,635, up from the previous CAD $10,000.

- **Eligible Institutions**: The list of designated learning institutions (DLIs) eligible for the SDS has been expanded. Students must be accepted into a full-time program at a DLI that is on the updated list.

## Advantages Over the Regular Visa Process

The SDS channel offers several advantages compared to the regular study permit application process:

- **Faster Processing**: SDS applications are typically processed more quickly than regular study permit applications, with a target processing time of 20 calendar days (when complete).

- **Simplified Requirements**: The SDS process requires fewer supporting documents, such as proof of funds and acceptance letter, but the requirements are more streamlined.

- **Higher Success Rate**: Applicants who meet the SDS eligibility criteria have a higher likelihood of their application being approved, as the program is designed for students who meet certain financial and language proficiency thresholds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the SDS available for all levels of study?

No, the SDS is available only for students applying to full-time programs at eligible post-secondary institutions.

### Can I apply for the SDS if I have a study permit application already in progress?

No, the SDS is only for new study permit applications. If you have an application in progress, you cannot switch to the SDS.

### What happens if my IELTS score is lower than the required band?

If your IELTS score does not meet the minimum requirement, you will not be eligible for the SDS. You may still apply through the regular study permit process.

### Do I need to pay the GIC before applying?

Yes, you must purchase a GIC from a participating Canadian financial institution and provide a proof of purchase as part of your application.

### How long does the SDS processing take?

While the target is 20 calendar days, actual processing times may vary depending on the volume of applications and other factors.
