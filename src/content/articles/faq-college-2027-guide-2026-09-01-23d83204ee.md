## Application Portals Open

Several Hong Kong universities have opened their online application systems for undergraduate admissions for the 2027 intake. The application portals are now accepting submissions from prospective students.

## Key Considerations

- Application deadlines and specific requirements vary by institution.
- Each university has its own application process and timeline.
- Prospective applicants should review the specific guidelines provided by each university before applying.

## Next Steps

- Visit the official websites of the individual universities to confirm their application deadlines and required documents.
- Prepare the necessary materials, such as academic transcripts and personal statements, well in advance.
- Submit applications through the designated online portals before the respective deadlines.

Note: The exact deadlines and requirements are not uniform across universities, so it is essential to check detailed information from each institution's official source.
